---
title: "Manage only what you declare"
slug: "manage-only-what-you-declare"
weight: 13
roman: "XIII"
part: "factor"
summary: "The tool touches only what you declare and deletes only what it owns."
---

A half-written configuration should never claim authority over what it does not mention. A field left out is a field the tool never reads or changes, and a delete is gated on an ownership marker the live resource carries, so dropping a line is not a purge and a resource the tool does not own is never swept away. This is what keeps adoption and a live source of truth survivable, because a newcomer's omission cannot become a mass deletion.
