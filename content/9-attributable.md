summary: "Every change traces to who made it and who approved it."
---

A newcomer's work is safe to accept only if you can see it afterward, every change tied to who made it, what they changed, and who approved it, and an agent's work is safe to accept on the same terms. The record is what turns "we let it make changes" into something you can audit.
